# Free-to-use visual inspiration sources (checked 2026-10-15)

"Free to download" is not the same as "free to use". This list separates sources whose images you
may actually reuse from sources that are only good for looking. Licences change; check the item
page before publishing anything.

## Free to reuse — ranked for art-direction value and clean rights

| # | Source | Good for | Licence reality |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | [Public Domain Review](https://publicdomainreview.org) | Curated, design-forward oddities with essays | Mostly public domain; each image carries a rights label — confirm with the holding institution ([rights labelling](https://publicdomainreview.org/rights-labelling-on-our-site/)) |
| 2 | [Smithsonian Open Access](https://www.si.edu/openaccess) (includes Cooper Hewitt design museum) | Posters, design objects, natural history, photography | CC0 where the media is flagged CC0; attribution appreciated, not required ([FAQ](https://www.si.edu/openaccess/faq)) |
| 3 | [The Met Open Access](https://www.metmuseum.org/hubs/open-access) | Fine art, fashion, photography, Asian art | CC0 for public-domain works (490,000+ images) |
| 4 | [Art Institute of Chicago](https://www.artic.edu/open-access) | Modernism, Japanese prints, photography | CC0 for public-domain works (50,000+); a caption is requested ([image licensing](https://www.artic.edu/image-licensing)) |
| 5 | [Rijksmuseum](https://www.rijksmuseum.nl/en/rijksstudio) | Dutch masters in extreme detail | Most digitised works public domain |
| 6 | [Cleveland Museum of Art](https://www.clevelandart.org/open-access) | Broad collection, print-size files | CC0 for works marked CC0 (over half the collection) |
| 7 | [National Gallery of Art](https://www.nga.gov/open-access-images.html) | American and European painting | CC0 for public-domain works |
| 8 | [Biodiversity Heritage Library on Flickr](https://www.flickr.com/photos/biodivlibrary/) | Botanical and zoological plates | Mostly public domain / "no known restrictions"; check each image |
| 9 | [Old Book Illustrations](https://www.oldbookillustrations.com) | Engravings and line art | Public-domain status is the site's estimate; no bulk downloading ([terms](https://www.oldbookillustrations.com/terms-of-use/)) |
| 10 | [Library of Congress — Free to Use and Reuse](https://www.loc.gov/free-to-use/) | Themed sets: posters, maps, photographs | Sets selected as free of known restrictions |
| 11 | [NASA Image and Video Library](https://images.nasa.gov) | Space and scientific imagery | Generally not copyrighted; no logos, no implied endorsement ([guidelines](https://www.nasa.gov/nasa-brand-center/images-and-media/)) |
| 12 | [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org) | Everything, including contemporary photography | Mixed licences — check each file (CC0, public domain, CC BY, CC BY-SA…) |
| 13 | [Openverse](https://openverse.org) | One search across many Creative Commons sources | Mixed CC licences; filter to CC0 / public domain mark; Openverse does not verify licences itself |
| 14 | [Unsplash](https://unsplash.com) | Contemporary editorial photography | Free licence for ordinary use, but API terms require hotlinking and its terms bar use in machine-learning datasets ([terms](https://unsplash.com/terms)) |
| 15 | [Pexels](https://www.pexels.com) | Contemporary stock photos and video | Free licence; bans large-scale collection for training or evaluating AI ([help](https://help.pexels.com/hc/en-us/articles/900005880463)) |

## Inspiration only — NOT free to use

These are excellent for mood and taste, but the images belong to their creators. Look, save
links, credit — do not reuse.

| Source | Why it is popular | Rights |
|---|---|---|
| [Cosmos](https://www.cosmos.so) | The current favourite for art-direction moodboards | Treat everything as copyrighted |
| [Are.na](https://www.are.na) | Thoughtful research boards | No licence to reuse others' content; scraping restricted ([terms](https://www.are.na/terms)) |
| [Savee](https://savee.com) | Art-direction saves | Savee cannot license the images ([terms](https://www.savee.com/terms)) |
| [Pinterest](https://www.pinterest.com) | Mass-market moodboards | Automated collection banned ([terms](https://policy.pinterest.com/en/terms-of-service)) |

## What Visual Atlas uses automatically

Visual Atlas collects only from sources whose per-item licence it can check in code and whose
terms allow storing, displaying and analysing the image: The Met, Art Institute of Chicago,
Cleveland Museum of Art, Smithsonian Open Access and Wikimedia Commons (CC0, public domain and
CC BY files only). Unsplash, Pexels and Pixabay stay disabled because their API or AI terms do not
clearly allow this use. The live policy, with evidence links, is `atlas/data/source_policies.json`.
